Request Pruner removes configurable browser headers and analytics cookies from the request in Repeater or Proxy Intercept without sending HTTP requests.

Right-click the request editor in Repeater or Proxy Intercept and select "Remove specified headers and cookies". The Request Pruner tab contains header and cookie lists with comments explaining each default entry. Add exact names or prefix* rules, toggle entries, and edit names and comments on custom entries. Header names ignore case; cookie names are case-sensitive.

Cookie rules are grouped by vendor. Click a group to expand it, or toggle the whole group with its checkbox. Analytics and advertising cookies are enabled by default; identity, consent, and A/B testing cookies are off. Cloudflare cookies are listed: non-challenge cookies are enabled, while challenge and security-related cookies are off. Removing enabled Cloudflare cookies may change origin affinity, offline caching, or queue position. Other vendors' bot-defense cookies remain excluded. Settings and custom comments are saved in the Burp project.

Some servers depend on removed headers or cookies, so responses may change. The extension retains the request method, URL, parameters, and body. Requires Java 21.
